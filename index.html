03 — The mark

Four characters, one shape.

BP is Big Project. 96 is the founder's birth year.

Put them side by side and they turn out to be the same thing: a ring and a stem. Flip b upside down and it becomes p. Turn 9 half a circle and it becomes 6. Turn the whole 96 and it still reads 96.

The logo is drawn from that single module, so every character has the same weight, the same ring and the same stem.
