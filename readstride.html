Your own library

Import TXT files, keep reading positions and review your daily and weekly practice. Core reading works offline.

Seven interface languages

English, Turkish, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian, German and French. No app account required.

Optional extras.

Basic reading, exercises and classic readings are free. Some extra texts can be opened temporarily with an optional rewarded ad. Lifetime Premium removes ads and unlocks the extra texts included in the product, with a one-time Google Play purchase.

The updated version is in internal testing. Ad availability depends on consent, connection and AdMob readiness. Results are for personal practice; no particular reading speed or improvement is guaranteed.
