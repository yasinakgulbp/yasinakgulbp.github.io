Akış 1.0.0 — English

Reading texts, settings, reading positions and practice history stay on your device and are not sent to a developer server. No app account, ads, in-app payments or analytics SDK is used. No camera, microphone, contacts or location permission is required. Files are read and written only through your system file picker choices. You control exported backups and any cloud service used to store them. Automatic cloud sync and Android system backup are disabled. Reset local reading data in Settings; exported files must be removed separately. Intended for ages 13 and up. Contact: bp96studio@gmail.com.

Akış 1.1 · Privacy policy (English) · 18 October 2026

BP96 STUDIO — bp96studio@gmail.com

Your optional name, language and reading settings, imported texts, reading positions and practice history stay on your device. They are not sent to a developer server. No app account, camera, microphone, contacts or location permission is required.

Ads: No ads are requested until you choose an age group. We do not collect your date of birth; your choice stays on the device. Google AdMob may process IP addresses, device/advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic data. Non-personalized ads can also process data. Google UMP presents privacy choices where required. You can revisit ad privacy choices in Settings. Ad requests stop when Premium is active. Google policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Premium: Google Play handles payments; we receive no card details. The product ID and signed purchase receipt are kept in secure device storage to verify access. The offline receipt cache lasts up to 24 hours; internet may then be required. JSON backups do not grant Premium. Restore purchases using the same Google Play account.

Files are read or written only through your system picker choices. A cloud backup folder is subject to its provider’s terms. There is no automatic cloud sync or Android system backup. Reset in Settings deletes reading records, but not exported files or Google Play purchase history. Ad counters and timed access are stored separately on the device; clear app data in Android settings to remove them. Intended for ages 13 and up. Changes will be reflected in this policy.
